## Verdict in short

- **No single root cause.** The fixes fall into 3 structural roots and 1 unrelated group (§ Fix history).
  1. The subscription layer has its own ignore list.
  2. Every detach and re-attach costs a 33 s crawl of the whole tree.
  3. The recovery model is built for macOS FSEvents. On Linux, Parcel's inotify backend fails in 3 ways that gmax neither detects nor repairs.
- **The lead is partly right.** At the subscription layer the watcher and the indexer use 2 different lists. At the event and indexing layer they share 1 source, `ProjectFilePolicy`. Unifying the lists would have prevented 15 of the 24 Linux poll-mode falls, but not the other 9 (§ Discriminating check).
- **The biggest live defect on 0.26.113 is correctness, not CPU.** Directories created with `mkdir -p` after the subscribe never get a watch. 2 such directories exist now, and their 16 indexable files are missing from the index while `gmax status` reports the watcher as native and healthy (§ Live state).

## 4. The discriminating check: does the watcher share the indexer's ignore source?

### The indexer's walk

- **`walk`/`walkDirectory`** (`src/lib/index/walker.ts:34-109`) classifies each directory with `ProjectFilePolicy.classifyDirectory` and each file with `classifyFile`.
- **`ProjectFilePolicy`** (`src/lib/index/file-policy.ts:43-62,118-153,213-241`) uses 3 sources:
  1. `DEFAULT_IGNORE_PATTERNS` (`src/lib/index/ignore-patterns.ts:36-119`) plus `.git` and `.gmax`;
  2. every `.gitignore` and `.gmaxignore` from the root down to the path, each scoped to its own directory;
  3. `isIndexableFile` and size limits.
- **The same walk serves the daemon's catch-up** (`watcher-manager.ts:740` on, `catchupScan`) and the standalone `reconcile` (`watcher.ts:214`).

### What the watcher subscribes to

- **`subscribeWithNativeExclusions`** (`watcher-ignore.ts:211-241`) passes Parcel 3 sets:
  1. `WATCHER_IGNORE_GLOBS` (`watcher.ts:76-92`): a second hand-written name list, `IGNORED_DIR_NAMES` (`watcher.ts:48-73`), plus temp/swap globs and `GENERATED_SOURCE_PATTERNS` (shared with the indexer, `ignore-patterns.ts:12-33`);
  2. root `.gmaxignore` only, positive simple lines only, with any `!` disabling the whole file (`watcher-ignore.ts:10-56`). The comment at `:39-40` says "Nested scopes and .gitignore remain authoritative in file policy";
  3. at most 8 literal directories (`watcher-ignore.ts:58,67-164`). These are discovered by asking `ProjectFilePolicy.classifyDirectory`, bounded to 512 directories, 16,384 entries, depth 4 and 1 s.

### What the watcher filters after delivery

`ProjectBatchProcessor.handleFileEvent` (`src/lib/index/batch-processor.ts:170-240`) applies 3 filters:
- `isIndexableFile`;
- `ProjectFilePolicy.isIgnoredByLoadedPolicy` (`file-policy.ts:80-96`), which drops paths that were never indexed;
- then `classifyFile` before indexing.

### Verdict

**The lead is partly right.**

- **Subscription layer: 2 sources.** The 2 hand lists drift:
  - `.turbo` is only in the watcher list (`watcher.ts:68`);
  - `.claude` (`ignore-patterns.ts:70`), `**/fixtures/**`, `**/benchmark/**`, `**/testdata/**`, `**/__snapshots__/**`, `**/.idea/**` and `**/.vscode/**` are only in the indexer list.
- **No `.gitignore` reaches the subscription.** Neither the root nor any nested one does, except through the 8 literals, which on this repo add only the root `.dev`.
- **Event and index layer: 1 source** (`ProjectFilePolicy`), so the mismatch costs watches and races, not wrong index content.
- **What the lead got wrong:**
  - the 8 literals are discovered from file policy, not hardcoded;
  - there are 23 hardcoded name globs as well.
- **Measured cost of the drift:**
  - 356 gitignored and about 220 indexer-ignored directories watched (the 2 sets overlap);
  - 15 of the 24 Linux poll-mode falls started in gitignored trees.
- **What unifying the lists would not fix:**
  - the other 9 races in the indexed `docs/plans/assets` tree;
  - the new-directory miss.

## 5. Other watcher defects, ranked

### By CPU and fan impact

1. **Re-watch churn (fixed in 0.26.113 for bounded maintenance; keep observing).** 2,543 platform unwatches since 2026-10-29 (Pacific), each a 33 s crawl plus a full catch-up walk (17.4 s). The remaining `quiesceAll` callers are:
   - service pause (`daemon.ts:1068`);
   - repair (`:2671`);
   - shutdown (`:3679`);
   - maintenance while the service is paused (`:1562`, `:1689`).

   All are rare by design.
2. **Linux subscribe `ENOENT` drops straight into poll mode.**
   - **The code:** `watchProject` primes `watcherFailCount` to 1,000 (`watcher-manager.ts:312-318`), so a transient race in a 33 s crawl window counts as a hard kernel failure.
   - **The cost now:** with the churn gone, an episode lasts at least 15 min until the probe (`FSEVENTS_RECOVERY_INTERVAL_MS`, `:34`), plus a 5 min health window (`:35`, `:676`). That means a 17 s full walk every 5 min (`:509-541`), an extra 33 s crawl per probe, and live edits delayed up to 5 min.
   - **The race path:** Parcel throws on any `inotify_add_watch` failure in subscribe (`InotifyBackend.cc:67-77`) and on any `openat` error but `EACCES` in the crawl (`unix/legacy.cc:30-36,66-68`).
   - **Seen 24 times; 0 on 0.26.113 so far,** in 1 subscribe.
3. **A policy-file trigger matches by basename anywhere in the tree.**
   - **The code:** `isPolicyFile` checks only `path.basename` (`file-policy.ts:64-66`), and `handleFileEvent` tests it before any ignore check (`batch-processor.ts:191-195`). Any `.gitignore` written inside a watched but ignored tree therefore clears the whole ignore cache.
   - **The trigger exists here:** 5 such files sit in ignored watched dirs, among them `services/*/.ruff_cache/.gitignore` (Ruff's cache folder; `.ruff_cache` is in neither list) and `.runlist/.gitignore`.
   - **The cost per trigger:** the daemon then always re-subscribes (`onPolicyChange`, `watcher-manager.ts:188-205`, to `subscribeWatcherNow` `:394-410`, with no identity check). That is up to 3 Parcel crawls (`watcher-ignore.ts:219-240`), each another race window, plus a full catch-up.
   - **Latent:** `scanCount` 1 shows it has not fired on 0.26.113.
4. **The watched tree is about 88% `docs/plans/assets`** (29,130 directories). File globs in `.gmaxignore` cannot exclude folders, so every subscribe crawls them. This is a project choice; directory rules would shrink the 33 s, but the owner decides whether those folders need live watching.

   - **Newly exposed by 0.26.113.**
   - **Same blind spot elsewhere:** the same Parcel path loses files written into a new directory before its watch exists.
2. **Linux overflow is invisible.** Parcel drops `IN_Q_OVERFLOW` silently (`InotifyBackend.cc:120-123`; `max_queued_events` is 16,384 per `/proc/sys/fs/inotify/max_queued_events`). gmax's gap handling matches FSEvents strings only (`watcher-errors.ts:3-11`). So a Linux drop never triggers reconciliation, and `gapCount` stays at 0. Not measured.
3. **Health reporting cannot show items 1 and 2.** `watcherMode` is "native" whenever there is no poll interval, recovery or pending overflow (`watcher-manager.ts:140-157`). On Linux, nothing ever sets those for these 2 cases.
4. **The 2 implementations have diverged.**
   - The daemon re-subscribes on every policy event without comparing identity (`watcher-manager.ts:394-410`); the standalone watcher compares first (`watcher.ts:126-131`).
   - The standalone watcher has no poll-mode fallback, so a startup `ENOENT` rejects `startWatcher` (`watcher.ts:325`). The effect on a standalone `gmax watch` process was not run.

## 6. Proposed fix shape (no code)

4 tasks, each independently shippable, in this order:

1. **Transient subscribe failures are retried, not escalated.**
   - Treat `ENOENT`/`ENOTDIR` from `inotify_add_watch` or the crawl, on a path inside the root, as transient. Retry the subscribe 2 to 3 times with seconds of backoff before poll mode, and log the path.
   - On Linux, probe out of poll mode on the first 5-minute poll rather than at 15 minutes.
   - Scope: `watcher-manager.ts` `watchProject`, `recoverWatcher` and `schedulePollModeRecovery`; tests with a fake subscribe that fails once.
2. **New-directory coverage on Linux.**
   - On a create event for a directory, run a scoped `walk` of that directory through `ProjectFilePolicy`, so files already inside are indexed.
   - If that walk finds subdirectories, the watch tree is incomplete, so schedule 1 debounced re-subscribe (or patch Parcel's inotify backend to crawl new directories recursively and to report `IN_Q_OVERFLOW` as an error, the way FSEvents gaps are).
   - Map an overflow error into the existing gap catch-up.
   - **This collides with Root B:** each re-subscribe is another 33 s crawl and another `ENOENT` window. Here, 20 non-ignored directories were created in the 4 h 13 min after the 16:32:12 PST subscribe (`find <repo> -xdev -type d -newerBt '2026-11-09 16:32:12'`, minus watcher-ignored names and `git check-ignore`), and 2 of them missed a watch. So a re-subscribe would be needed about twice in that window. A Parcel patch avoids that cost; a re-subscribe must be debounced and only run when the scoped walk finds a subdirectory.
   - Patching Parcel versus re-subscribing is the design question in this task; both stay inside the "no new watcher migration" line at `gmax-delivery.md:7179`.
3. **1 ignore source for the subscription.**
   - Build the Parcel ignore set from `ProjectFilePolicy`'s sources: `DEFAULT_IGNORE_PATTERNS` directory names, plus root and nested `.gitignore`/`.gmaxignore` directory rules translated with their scope prefix, keeping the existing "skip negations and unsafe syntax" rule.
   - Delete `IGNORED_DIR_NAMES`.
   - Keep the 8-literal cap only on macOS.
   - Make `isPolicyFile` ignore policy files inside directories the loaded policy excludes.
   - Make the daemon skip a re-subscribe when the ignore identity is unchanged, as the standalone watcher does.
   - A drift guard test should assert that every indexer directory exclusion is also a subscription exclusion.
4. **1 watcher implementation.** Move subscribe, recover and reconcile into one module that both `startWatcher` and `WatcherManager` use, so tasks 1 to 3 land once. This is the structural refactor. `watcher-manager.ts` is 1,176 lines, so dead code is removed first per the repo rule.

Not a gmax task: whether `docs/plans/assets` directories need live watching at all. That is a platform `.gmaxignore` choice for the owner, and it decides most of the 33 s crawl.
